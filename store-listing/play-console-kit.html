4. Icon prompt optional upgrade

For a more polished icon than the geometric placeholder above — feed this to an AI image tool (Midjourney, Gemini, DALL·E) or a designer, then re-run the icon step below with the result.

Prompt
A minimal, modern flat-vector app icon for "SportsFund", a sports court-booking and cost-splitting app. No text or lettering anywhere. Solid dark navy background (#0A0F1E). One bold sports ball drawn only in clean outline/line-art strokes, in neon lime-green (#CCFF00), with geometric curved seam lines like a basketball or volleyball. Optionally a small, subtle coin or dollar-sign accent overlapping the ball to hint at splitting costs, without cluttering the mark. Centered composition with generous padding — keep all artwork inside the middle 66% of the canvas so it survives a circular crop and Android's adaptive-icon mask. Square canvas, 1024x1024px, fully opaque (no transparency), no drop shadows, no busy gradients, no rounded-square background shape (the store applies its own mask). Style reference: confident flat sports-tech branding like Strava or Whoop, crossed with a dark fintech palette.
Technical constraints to keep whatever you generate: 512×512 for the Play Store hi-res icon (32-bit PNG, alpha OK) · Android adaptive icon safe zone is the center ~66% · iOS App Store icon must be 1024×1024 with zero transparency.
