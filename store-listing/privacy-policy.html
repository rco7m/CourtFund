    01 Overview

    SportsFund ("we," "our," "us") is a mobile app for finding sports venues, booking sessions, splitting costs with friends, and tracking your gear and activity. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the SportsFund app on Android and iOS.

    This document is a plain-language draft generated for the app's first Play Console / App Store submission. Have a lawyer review it against your actual launch jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR if you'll have EU users, CCPA for California) before relying on it as your final policy.

    02 Information we collect

    Account information

    When you create an account we collect your email address, a password (stored as a salted hash by our authentication provider, never in plain text), and an optional display name and profile photo.

    Location

    If you grant permission, we use your device's precise or approximate location to search for nearby courts and sports venues. Location is used to run that search and is not stored on our servers; your last-selected venue is cached on your own device so the app can reopen where you left off.

    Content you create

    To provide the app's core features, we store the activity you generate inside it:

    • Booking, schedule, and session-log entries
    • Expenses you log and cost-splits you create or are added to, and their dollar amounts
    • Gear you track
    • Friend connections between SportsFund accounts
    • Messages you send to the in-app AI assistant

    Automatically collected information

    Our backend provider (Supabase) automatically logs standard technical data for security and reliability, such as IP address, device/OS version, and request timestamps.

    03 How we use your information

    • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in
    • To show nearby venues and power booking, scheduling, and cost-splitting
    • To let friends you connect with see shared bookings and split expenses
    • To respond to messages you send the in-app AI assistant
    • To provide customer support when you email us
    • To keep the service secure and diagnose technical problems

    We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal information.

    04 Third parties we work with

    We use a small number of service providers to run the app. None of them are permitted to use your data for their own advertising.

    Supabase — auth & database Google Gemini API — AI assistant OpenStreetMap Overpass — venue search

    When you use the AI assistant, your message text is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. When you search for venues, your coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API to find nearby places — no account information is included in that request.

    05 Sharing & disclosure

    We share information only:

    • With the service providers above, solely to operate the app
    • With friends you connect with, limited to what a shared booking or split reasonably shows them (e.g. your name and your share of a cost)
    • If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of SportsFund or our users
    • If SportsFund is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale — you'd be notified first

    06 Data retention & deletion

    We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account is active, so the app keeps working the way you'd expect. You can delete individual entries (bookings, expenses, gear) inside the app at any time. To delete your account and its data entirely, use Profile → Delete account in the app, or email sportsfund.all@gmail.com — we'll complete deletion within 30 days, except where we're required to retain limited records by law.

    07 Your rights & choices

    • Access & correction — view and edit your profile and activity directly in the app.
    • Deletion — request account deletion at any time (see above).
    • Location — deny or revoke location permission any time in your device settings; venue search just won't be able to find nearby places.
    • Portability & objection — where applicable under your local law (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), email us to request a copy of your data or object to specific processing.

    08 Children's privacy

    SportsFund is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

    09 Security

    We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, and access controls on our database — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

    10 International transfers

    Our service providers may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence. By using SportsFund, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

    11 Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes, notify you in the app.
